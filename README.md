# Math by Tyler — Redesign

This is a redesigned static site for mathbytyler.com with a different visual language and page structure.

## Included
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js
- assets/favicon.svg

## Notes
- Public contact information and forms have been intentionally removed to reduce spam and scraper harvesting.
- The site now states that tutoring is available by referral without publishing an email address.
- Replace the photo placeholder with a real photo of Tyler

- Experience language updated to reflect 20+ years of tutoring, beginning in Tyler's teens.

- About section now mentions Tyler's Mechanical Engineering study at the University of South Florida.
- Consultation form restored using the same Formspree endpoint as The Farmers Brown: xwleegod.
- No public email address is displayed on the site.

- Added website-ready portrait to About section.
- Updated form headline to “How Can I Help?”
- Updated base tutoring rate to $80/hour.

- Made Art of Problem Solving contest-math teaching experience explicit.
- Expanded student range to include regular college-level tutoring and Calculus.
- Added an exploratory small-group classes section and group-class inquiry option.

- Replaced the sparse hero math panel with the denser generated mathematics artwork.
