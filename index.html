Approach

The lesson on the page is not always the lesson the student needs.

A student struggling with Geometry may really be missing algebra fluency. A student who appears to “understand the homework” may actually be relying on pattern-matching.

My job is to identify what is actually happening, not just repeat the classroom lesson louder. That means asking good questions, watching how a student thinks, and rebuilding understanding from the point where it starts to wobble.

What sessions look like

Not a canned curriculum. Not homework babysitting.

Sessions are built around diagnosis, explanation, guided practice, and gradually shifting more of the thinking onto the student.

1

Start with context

What course is the student taking? What feels hard? What outcome are we aiming for?

2

Look under the surface

I pay attention to process, misconceptions, and habits—not just whether the answer is right.

3

Build understanding

We focus on the ideas and decision-making that let students handle unfamiliar problems better.

4

Aim for independence

The goal is not dependence on a tutor. The goal is confidence and durable understanding.

FAQ

Before you reach out.

Who do you work with?

I primarily work with middle-school and high-school students, from foundational math through advanced coursework and enrichment.

Do you tutor online or in person?

Both. Most students can be served online. Limited in-person sessions are available in the Tampa area.

Can you guarantee a grade or score?

No responsible tutor can promise a specific grade or placement result. What I can do is improve understanding, preparation, and habits.

Can you help with homework?

Yes—but the goal is not just to finish tonight’s work. We use current assignments to expose understanding and strengthen it.
