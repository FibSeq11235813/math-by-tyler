A different kind of tutoring

The current lesson isn't always the real problem.

A student struggling with Algebra II may have gaps in Algebra I. A student who “can't solve equations” may actually be getting tripped up by fractions, negatives, or order of operations.

I work one-on-one with students to find the point where understanding breaks down, rebuild what’s missing, and then move forward with confidence. For advanced students, we can go the other direction entirely—deeper problems, richer reasoning, and genuinely challenging mathematics.

How I teach

Homework help is useful.
Independence is better.

I’m not interested in becoming the voice that sits beside a student forever saying, “Okay, now do this step.”

Good tutoring should gradually make the tutor less necessary. That means students need to explain their thinking, make decisions, try approaches, recover from mistakes, and understand why a method works—not merely copy it.

1

We talk.

Start with a short consultation about what’s going on and what you want to accomplish.

2

I assess.

I pay attention to how the student thinks, not just whether the final answer is right.

3

We build.

Sessions target the student’s actual needs—not a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

4

They take over.

The long-term goal is stronger reasoning, confidence, and independence.

FAQ

Before we get started.

Still not sure whether I’m the right fit? That’s exactly what the free consultation is for.

Do you tutor online or in person?

Both. Most sessions can be handled online using an interactive digital workspace. Limited in-person tutoring is available in the Tampa area.

What ages and math levels do you work with?

I primarily work with middle-school and high-school students, including students rebuilding foundational skills and advanced students seeking enrichment.

Can you help with tonight’s homework?

Yes—but the goal is bigger than finishing an assignment. I use the current work to identify misconceptions, build understanding, and help students become more independent.

Can you guarantee a certain grade or test score?

No. Tutoring can improve understanding and preparation, but no responsible tutor can guarantee a specific grade, placement, or test result.

What happens during the free consultation?

We’ll spend about 15 minutes discussing the student’s current course, where they’re struggling or under-challenged, and what you’d like tutoring to accomplish.
